AI Collaboration

An overview of how I use AI to accelerate my workflow.

This project is an exploration of systems engineering and a showcase of my technical design decisions. To maximize my development velocity, I treat LLMs as a high-powered pairing partner and architectural sounding board. This is how I have used AI in this project:

  1. Research: Sifting through and condensing decades of historical computing documentation and fundamentals into clear consumable pieces of information.
  2. Architecture: Brainstorming design decisions, playing devil’s advocate, and rigorously reviewing core system layouts.
  3. Scaffolding: Generating clean boilerplate code for verified designs and architectural features.
  4. Grunt Work: Implementing isolated, well-defined parts of logic once the overarching architectural boundaries are established.
  5. Documentation: Reviewing and fine-tuning the copy for technical documentation and journal entries to ensure consistency and clarity.

While AI serves as an accelerator, I am ultimately responsible for the vision, architecture, engineering trade-offs, logic, and execution. Every line of code and architectural decision in this repository has been thoroughly vetted, analyzed, and integrated by hand.
